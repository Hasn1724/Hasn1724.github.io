02 / A little about me

Curious by
nature.

I’m a designer and developer who loves making useful things feel a little more human. I move between visual design and code, bringing ideas to life from the first sketch to the final, polished pixel.

My approach is collaborative, detail-minded, and always curious. Good work should look considered, feel effortless, and work beautifully for everyone.

When I’m away from my screen, I’m probably collecting inspiration, trying something new, or enjoying a really good cup of coffee.
